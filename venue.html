About the venue

Wasing is a traditional farm hamlet in the Berkshire countryside, sitting next to the 15th-century Parish Church of St Nicholas.

It’s still a working farm, and the beef served on the menu comes from their own herd of Sussex cattle, raised on the estate.

Ceremony & reception

The ceremony and reception both take place at Wasing Park. Guests are welcome to arrive from midday and head to the Stables bar, which will be open until half an hour before the wedding ceremony.
