Venue & Details

About the venue

Wasing is a restored traditional farm hamlet in the Berkshire countryside, sitting right next to the 15th-century Parish Church of St Nicholas.

It's still a working farm, with the beef on the menu coming from their own herd of Sussex cattle, raised on the estate.

Ceremony & reception

The ceremony and reception both take place at Wasing Park. Guest can arrive from midday and head to the the Stables bar, which will be open until half an hour before the wedding ceremony.
