Venue & Details

About the venue

Wasing is a restored traditional farm hamlet in the Berkshire countryside, sitting right next to the 15th-century Parish Church of St Nicholas.

It's still a proper working farm — the beef on the menu comes from their own herd of Sussex cattle, raised right here on the estate.

Ceremony & reception

The ceremony and reception both take place at Wasing Park. The bar opens from midday and is available to all guests, until hald an hour before the wedding ceremony. Complimentary tea and coffee is also available.
