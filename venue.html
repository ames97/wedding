Venue & Details

About the venue

Wasing is a working farm and hamlet in the Berkshire countryside, sitting right next to the 15th-century Parish Church of St Nicholas.

It's still a proper working farm — the beef on the menu comes from their own herd of Sussex cattle, raised right here on the estate.

Ceremony & reception

The ceremony and reception both take place at Wasing Park. Guests can arrive from midday and head to the Stables bar, which will be open until half an hour before the wedding ceremony.
