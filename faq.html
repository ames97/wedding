Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What's the dress code?

Garden Formal. The venue has gravel walkways, so flat or block-heeled shoes are a good idea.

Can I get changed at the venue?

If you wish to change at the venue, please arrive at least 1 hour before the ceremony. En-route hotels like Premier Inn also offer an early check-in option as an alternative. Guests can change in the groom's room and leave bags ___

Where can I leave my coat?

There is a cloakroom situated in the entrance lobby to the Castle Bar, where guests will gather before the start of the ceremony.

Will there be parking?

There are plenty of parking spaces available and cars can be left overnight, for collection the next morning by latest 10:30 am. See the Travel page for the venue address and directions to the car park.

Will there be an open bar?

Drinks will be provided during the reception, toasting and wedding breakfast. For the evening party, additional drinks can be purchased at the bar. Please let us know in the RSVP form if you would like non-alcoholic drinks for the reception and meal.

Can I smoke?

Guests can smoke in designated areas outside where receptacles will be provided.

Does the venue cater for dietary requirements?

Please list any food intolerances or allergies in your RSVP form. You will also be able to select a vegeterian meal option or children's menu if required.

Is there a gift registry?

Your presence is most important to us! But if you’d like to give us a gift, we’d love a contribution towards our honeymoon.
