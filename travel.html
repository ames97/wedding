By car

On arrival, continue straight on from the top of the drive, over the crossroads, taking the left-hand turn into the main car park. Take the path from the car park leading to the venue.
Cars can be left overnight, to be collected by 10:30 a.m. the next morning.

By train

From London

Reading is the closest mainline train station to the venue with a reliable taxi rank and Uber availability. The taxi journey should take approximately 25 minutes.
There are direct trains which run every 15 minutes from Paddington station and take approximately 25 minutes. Book via the trainline app or buy tickets at a self-serve kiosk at the station. The Elizabeth line also runs to Reading and takes around 45 minutes, with trains scheduled every 10 minutes.

From Brighton

The quickest route is via either Reading or Basingstoke station, which are both around a 25 minute taxi ride from the venue. Both stations have Uber and taxi ranks, with plenty of hotel options offering early check in.
Alternatively, transfer at Reading to Aldermaston station and pre-book a taxi from there (5 min).
